Ride left into a kicker and press S or A just before the lip for extra pop. Rails grind like ledges. Near a quarter pipe's lip, W is a rock to fakie (W+Left nose stall, W+Right tail stall, W+Up axle stall, W+Down FS axle stall); hold W to hold a stall, S pops you out. Hit a quarter pipe fast enough and you launch off its lip. Spins need S: press S together with Up (frontside) or Down (backside) to spin 180, keep holding the arrow to go on to 360, 540, 720 and 900.

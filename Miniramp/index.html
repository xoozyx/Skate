Left/right: pump, and lean in the air. Up/down: ride toward the back or front of the ramp. Esc or the MENU button returns to the main menu. A kickflip (A+right heelflip, A+down shove-it, A+up FS shove-it): hold A or tap it again to keep flipping (double, triple, quad flips; 360, 540... shove-its). D grab (D+down indy, D+up melon, D+right nose, D+left tail). S spin: hold S to keep spinning (every 180 counts, up to 1440) and release to finish the turn; each tap also adds 180. Near the coping press W. If you are moving toward the back or front of the ramp (up/down while riding or in the air), W = 50-50 grind, W+right = nose slide, W+left = tail slide: they slide along the coping. If you are not moving that way, W = rock stall, W+right = nose stall, W+left = tail stall (keep the balance marker off the far end with left/right). Release W to drop in. After START you pick a level (big or half-size ramp), then your gear. BG IMAGE loads your own 240x138 PNG as the background.
